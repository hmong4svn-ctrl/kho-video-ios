Kho Video is a Hmong-language video editing tool. The interface is in Hmong (RPA), so
here is the exact path for each item App Review needs to check.

HOW TO DELETE THE ACCOUNT (Guideline 5.1.1(v))
1. Tap the account icon in the top-right corner of the home screen.
2. Tap "Rho tawm account" (Delete Account) - it is the last item in that panel.
3. Type RHO TAWM in the confirmation box, then confirm.
The account and all of its videos are deleted immediately; no email request is needed.
The same button is also reachable from the footer of the home screen.

HOW TO REPORT CONTENT (Guideline 1.2)
Tap the flag icon "Ceeb toom" (Report) in the footer of the home screen, choose a reason
and submit. Reports reach our team and are handled within 24 hours.

IN-APP PURCHASE (Guideline 3.1.1)
The app sells two monthly subscriptions, both available through In-App Purchase:
  com.hmongx.khovideo.pob1  - 100 edited videos per month, 720p export
  com.hmongx.khovideo.pob2  - 200 edited videos per month, 1080p export
Free users can edit 5 videos to try the app. Nothing is sold outside of In-App Purchase
inside the iOS app.

HOW TO TEST THE APP
1. Sign in with Apple (or use the demo account below).
2. On the home screen tap "Rho video tuaj" and pick any video from the photo library.
3. Choose one of the three buttons, for example "Kho 1 zaj video".
4. The app returns the edited video in about 10-30 seconds depending on length.

SIGN-IN
Sign in with Apple, Google and Facebook are all available. They open in Safari and return
to the app automatically.

LANGUAGE NOTE
Hmong (RPA) has no App Store localization, so the interface is Hmong with Vietnamese
subtitles in places. This is intentional - our users are Hmong speakers in Vietnam and Laos.
